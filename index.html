🌐 Emerging World Trends
1
Capital Markets Split Into a “Barbell”
VC has bifurcated into massive late-stage AI mega-rounds at one end and disciplined early-stage “pre-consensus” bets at the other, with a hollowed-out middle. AI capital concentration is widening the gap between mega-cap AI and overlooked non-AI small/mid-caps — creating value dislocations in consumer, financials and healthcare.
2
“Crosscurrents of War and Technology”
The IMF (July WEO) projects 3.0% global growth for 2026, split between a Middle East energy shock hitting importers and an AI-driven investment boom lifting tech-integrated economies. The same force — the Iran war — is simultaneously the biggest downside (oil) and the reason AI-capex economies keep outperforming.
3
Defense Tech Goes Mainstream — and Maybe Bubbly
Once taboo in Silicon Valley, defense tech is now among the most sought-after categories: VCs deployed a record $19.8B across 262 deals in Q1 2026, already eclipsing 2025’s full-year record. Europe’s Helsing hit $18B (the continent’s largest-ever VC round). Fortune is already warning of a bubble — a signal to underwrite, not chase.
4
Stablecoins Become Systemic Rails — and a Capital Source
Stablecoins are now a ~$250B asset class powering treasury, payments and savings, with transfer volume that surpassed Visa + Mastercard in 2024. Notably, stablecoin profits are now flowing into physical-world tech (Tether backing Neura Robotics’ ~€1B raise) — a new capital cycle from digital rails into hard assets.
5
Trade Regionalizes Into Managed Tariff Blocs
With USMCA left unrenewed, Mexico tariffs heading to 30% by Aug 1, a live 15% US–EU deal and DST/rare-earth standoffs open, global trade is fracturing into managed regional blocs. Friend-shoring, domestic supply chains and pricing power outrank frictionless globalization.
