🌐 Emerging World Trends
1
From "Tokenmaxxing" to Efficiency
Enterprise AI spend is pivoting from raw token volume to cost-per-outcome. New model tiers (GPT-5.6 Terra at ~half of prior flagship cost, cheap Chinese open-weights) reward better context management and fewer retries. The buyer question is shifting from "biggest model" to "cheapest reliable result."
2
The Power-Constrained Supercycle
Electricity is now the binding ceiling on AI. Goldman forecasts data-center power demand up 50% by 2027 and up to 165% by decade-end; consumption could near 1,050 TWh in 2026 — a "fifth-largest country" by energy use. ~100 GW of new capacity lands by 2030 (14% CAGR) as hyperscalers commit ~$1T. Securing firm, carbon-free power is the new competitive moat — lifting utilities, uranium, transformers and grid interconnect.
3
Chinese Open-Weights Reach the Frontier
Moonshot's Kimi K3 (2.8T params, open-source, July 17) claims to beat Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 on coding/agents — with DeepSeek, Ernie 5.0 and Qwen (100M+ MAU) behind it. US firms keep adopting cheaper Chinese weights as frontier costs surge, turning open-weight commoditization into a national-security flashpoint that's drawing new US export-control threats.
4
The Agentic Economy Goes Institutional
Agents are moving from prototypes to authorized economic actors that initiate purchases and settle value. With stablecoin rails, agent-specific bank endpoints and protocol translators (UCP/ACP/AP2), machine-to-machine commerce is becoming an infrastructure layer, not a demo.
5
Trade Fragmentation as the New Normal
USMCA left to lapse, a 15% EU tariff, 100% digital-services-tax threats and Section 301 probes mark a permanent shift from rules-based trade to bilateral leverage. Reshoring, supply-chain redundancy and trade-compliance software are the durable beneficiaries.
