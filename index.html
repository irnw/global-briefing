Trade Policy — Trump Rebuilds Tariff Engine on Section 301 After Supreme Court Struck Down IEEPA; 60 Countries Face 10–12.5%
After the Supreme Court ruled Feb 20 (Learning Resources v. Trump) that IEEPA does NOT authorize sweeping open-ended tariffs, the administration is quietly rebuilding the wall on firmer legal ground.
