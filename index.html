Russia-Ukraine — Russian Advance Stalls as Ukraine’s UAV Doctrine Delivers
Russia’s territorial advance has suddenly stalled in June 2026, with Ukraine credited for steadily strengthening UAV capabilities that have neutralized Russia’s conventional advantages. Ukraine launched the largest attack on Moscow since 2022, demonstrating expanding long-range strike capability. A Russian airstrike on a Kyiv apartment complex killed at least 24 people. Ukrainian forces struck residential buildings and an oil refinery in Ryazan, Russia. European officials say Moscow’s escalating aggression reflects increasing military and economic difficulties — Russia facing pressure from both battlefield stalemate and a battered economy fueling domestic discontent. So what: The stall is the inflection point. Russia’s economic strain — now openly acknowledged by European intelligence — suggests the war of attrition is shifting. Investment angle: European defense spending (2.5%+ GDP) locked in for a decade. Drone/counter-drone companies (AeroVironment, Kratos) are the fastest-growing segment.
