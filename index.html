Trade — Twin Deadlines This Week: Section 122 Global 10% Tariff Expires July 24; USTR Faces July 20 to Finalize Section 301 Replacement (Proposed 12.5% on 46 Countries)
The tariff architecture is being rewritten in the next 10 days. So what: A pivotal week that hard-codes tariffs into durable statutory authority. Investment angle: Reinforces friend-shoring and pricing-power compounders; import-exposed autos/ag and Asian exporters face fresh uncertainty into the July 20–24 window.

The AI buildout is spreading beyond US/China to Southeast Asia (~$30B), the Gulf and India, while custom silicon (OpenAI, DeepSeek, Alibaba) and alt-GPU clouds fragment supply. Sovereign compute and power access become strategic national assets.
3
Trade Regionalizes into Tariff Blocs
With USMCA left unrenewed, a 15% US–EU tariff live, and DST/rare-earth standoffs open, global trade is fracturing into managed regional blocs. Friend-shoring, domestic supply chains and pricing power outrank frictionless globalization.

Tesla’s Optimus V3 (22-DoF hands, AI5 chip, Grok voice) enters low-volume production at Fremont this summer — factory-only for now, with first enterprise sales targeted late 2026 and consumer availability (~$25–40k) end-2027. Global CPI rising to 4.7% (2026), the Warsh Fed lifting its PCE forecast to 3.6%, and ECB/BoJ hikes entrench a higher-cost regime. Capital favors cash flow, quality balance sheets and real assets over long-duration bets priced for cuts.
